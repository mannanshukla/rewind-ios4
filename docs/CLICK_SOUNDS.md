# Click sound recordings

Rewind switches click recordings with the selected skin. The previous shared sound was iOS keyboard sound 1104. These are short acoustic excerpts from actual devices, not synthesized pitches or extracted firmware audio.

The Nano source shows a third-generation Nano. The Original source shows the early mechanical-wheel body. The Mini source is explicitly a second-generation Mini; it represents the Mini family and is not claimed as a verified first-generation firmware match. The Classic source identifies an 80 GB Classic. Acoustic clickers vary between units and recording setups.

Processing: mono 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV; trim one transient, remove low-frequency room/voice/handling noise, add short edge fades, and balance level without pitch-shifting. High-pass cutoffs are Nano 2000 Hz, Original/Mini 900 Hz, Classic 1500 Hz. No narration or music is intentionally included. Original recordings remain credited to their uploaders.

The small resources are loaded once per skin through AudioServices and disposed when the player is released. The existing 45 ms click throttle and Clicker setting remain in control. No audio-session category or music playback queue is changed.
